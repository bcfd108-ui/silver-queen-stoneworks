Shipping

Every order ships from my bench in Lewiston, Idaho — the one stone in the listing photo, packed by hand. I ship to anywhere in the United States and Canada.

Cost

United States: $7.00 flat, and free on orders over $100. Canada: US$7.00 flat, and free on orders over US$100. One flat charge per order, whether it holds one stone or several.

When it ships

Orders ship the next business day. From here, transit runs 3–5 business days in the US and 5–10 business days to Canada.

The stone you get

There is no warehouse and no second copy out back. Every piece is one of a kind, the photo is the exact piece, and the stone in the photo is the stone that ships. When it sells, that particular piece is gone.
