Returns Policy

Every piece sold by Silver Queen Stoneworks is one of a kind. The photo on the listing is the exact piece you receive, and when a stone sells, that particular piece is gone. Because items cannot be restocked, all sales are final. We do not accept returns, exchanges, or refunds for change of mind, or because a buyer prefers a different pattern, color, size, or style.

Exceptions

If your item arrives damaged in shipping, or is not as described (the wrong item, or a piece that does not match the listing photo and description), contact us within 30 days of delivery at gene@silverqueenstoneworks.com or 208-400-1427. Please include your order number, photos of the item, and photos of the packing materials. Do not wear, resize, alter, or further polish the piece, and do not ship it back until we confirm. Once we confirm, we will provide the return address for shipping the piece back. In these exception cases, Silver Queen Stoneworks covers return shipping and will refund the item price and original outbound shipping after the piece is received back in the condition it was sent — or, if we both agree, a partial refund with the buyer keeping the piece. Refunds are issued to the original payment method typically within 5–7 business days of receipt.

Local meetup

Items may be shipped, or a local meetup can be arranged in the Lewiston-Clarkston area. Nobody is invited to a home or workshop address. Please inspect the piece at meetup before completing the purchase. Meetup purchases are final with no returns.

Questions

Call 208-400-1427 or email gene@silverqueenstoneworks.com.
